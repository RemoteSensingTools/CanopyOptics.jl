Dielectric leaf surfaces and energy-conserving BSDF

Standard plate models such as PROSPECT-5, PROSPECT-D and PROSPECT-PRO evaluate external surface entry with a fixed $40^\circ$ solid-angle cone (calctav(40, nr)) and assume bi-Lambertian outgoing reflectance and transmittance. That simplification introduces two physical limitations when comparing against directional or sphere-integrated measurements:

  1. Observation-geometry mismatch: Integrating spheres use $0^\circ$ or $8^\circ$ collimated entry, whereas the legacy $40^\circ$ cone lowers smooth-surface Fresnel reflectance by roughly $0.004$–$0.006$ across the visible spectrum.
  2. Non-conserving directional specular additions: Adding a microfacet BRDF term on top of an unmodified $40^\circ$ PROSPECT diffuse budget (as in uncoupled diffuse+specular overlays or PROSPECULAR at oblique solar zenith angles) double-counts boundary reflection and violates energy conservation ($R(\theta_i) + T(\theta_i) > 1$ in weakly absorbing NIR bands at $\theta_i \gtrsim 55^\circ$).

CanopyOptics separates the dielectric boundary operator (AbstractLeafSurface) from the internal mesophyll solver and couples both into an energy-conserving, Helmholtz-reciprocal 4×4 polarized BSDF (CoupledLeafBSDF).

Dielectric boundary models

Two boundary parameterizations subtype AbstractLeafSurface:

  • ConeSurface(α_deg, n_scale): analytical Stern–Allen cone-averaged smooth Fresnel interface (calctav_smooth), continuous down to exact normal incidence $\alpha = 0^\circ$ where $t_0(n) = 4n/(n+1)^2$.
  • RoughDielectricSurface(σ, n_scale): isotropic Beckmann microfacet slope distribution (beckmann_D) with separable Smith shadowing–masking (smith_beckmann_G1), integrated over $(\mu_o, \Delta\phi)$ via Gauss–Legendre quadrature (microfacet_directional_reflectance, microfacet_hemispherical_reflectance).

For any external boundary model with hemispherical external transmittance $t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}}(n_{\mathrm{eff}}, \sigma)$ into a lossless thin cuticle of effective index $n_{\mathrm{eff}} = n_{\mathrm{scale}} \, n_{\mathrm{surface}}(\lambda)$ overlying an aqueous mesophyll of index $n_r(\lambda)$, isothermal detailed balance (étendue conservation between air $n=1$ and mesophyll $n_r$) fixes the diffuse internal exit transmittance without free parameters:

\[t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = \frac{t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}}(n_{\mathrm{eff}}, \sigma)}{n_r^2} \in (0, 1), \qquad r_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = 1 - t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}}.\]

surface_interface_optics evaluates external entry and diffuse internal exit consistently for prospect_budget, leaf_sip_budget and dorsiventral_budget.

Epicuticular wax refractive-index dispersion

Aqueous mesophyll refractive index $n_r(\lambda)$ in OPTI_2021 drops to $1.27$–$1.34$ across $1900$–$2500\text{ nm}$, whereas epicuticular wax cuticles remain near $1.40$–$1.48$ across $400$–$2500\text{ nm}$ (Stuckens et al., 2009). load_wax_refractive_index averages the tabulated Stuckens et al. (2009) normal dispersion curve onto any wavelength grid so it can be supplied via n_surface (or per-face n_surface_adaxial and n_surface_abaxial):

using CanopyOptics, Unitful

λ_bnds = (399.5:1.0:2500.5)u"nm"
opti   = LeafOpticalProperties(λ_bnds)
n_wax  = load_wax_refractive_index(λ_bnds)

leaf = LeafProspectProProperties(N = 1.6, Ccab = 42.0, Cw = 0.013, Cm = 0.006)
surf = RoughDielectricSurface(0.16, 1.0)
bd   = prospect_budget(leaf, opti, surf; n_surface = n_wax)

In this thin lossless cuticle approximation, smooth internal wax–mesophyll Fresnel reflection $(n_{\mathrm{eff}} - n_r)^2 / (n_{\mathrm{eff}} + n_r)^2$ is neglected ($\le 0.05\%$ in the VNIR and $\le 0.5\%$ at $2450\text{ nm}$, with zero external-to-mesophyll total internal reflection whenever $n_r > 1$), while $t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}}(n_{\mathrm{eff}}, \sigma) / n_r^2$ preserves exact passive energy conservation and bi-hemispherical reciprocity $T_{\mathrm{ad}} = T_{\mathrm{ab}}$ even when only one face carries wax.

Coupled energy-conserving leaf BSDF

CoupledLeafBSDF and prepare_leaf_bsdf couple single-bounce polarized microfacet reflection (surface_brdf_mueller) directly to the angle-dependent entry transmittance $t_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mu_i) = 1 - R_{\mathrm{surf}}(\mu_i)$ of the underlying single-zone or two-zone DorsiventralLeafProperties mesophyll stack.

For an incident ray with cosine $\mu_i$ on face $s \in \{\text{adaxial}, \text{abaxial}\}$:

  1. Single-scattering surface reflection carries directional-hemispherical weight $R_{\mathrm{surf},s}(\mu_i)$ and 4×4 Mueller BRDF $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{surf},s}(\Omega_i, \Omega_o)$ rotated smoothly into the incoming and outgoing meridian-plane Stokes bases.
  2. Only the transmitted fraction $t_{\mathrm{ext},s}(\mu_i) = 1 - R_{\mathrm{surf},s}(\mu_i)$ enters the interior stack, where multiple scattering between the mesophyll and the diffuse inner boundaries $r_{\mathrm{int},s}$ produces escaping interior reflection $R_{\mathrm{vol},s}(\mu_i)$, transmission $T_{\mathrm{vol},s}(\mu_i)$, and material absorptance $A_s(\mu_i)$ satisfying $R_{\mathrm{surf},s}(\mu_i) + R_{\mathrm{vol},s}(\mu_i) + T_{\mathrm{vol},s}(\mu_i) + A_s(\mu_i) = 1$ identically for all $\mu_i \in (0, 1]$ (leaf_directional_budget).
  3. Interior reflection escapes across face $s$ with dimensionless normalized escape profile $E_s(\mu) = t_{\mathrm{ext},s}(\mu) / t_{\mathrm{hem,ext},s}$, giving interior BRDF contribution $f_{r,\mathrm{vol},s}(\Omega_i \to \Omega_o) = \frac{R_{\mathrm{vol,hem},s}}{\pi} E_s(\mu_i) E_s(\mu_o)$ and guaranteeing exact directional BRDF reciprocity $f_r(\Omega_i \to \Omega_o) = f_r(\Omega_o \to \Omega_i)$ and 4×4 Hovenier reciprocity $\mathbf{M}(\Omega_2, \Omega_1) = \mathbf{P}\,\mathbf{M}(\Omega_1, \Omega_2)^\top \mathbf{P}$ with $\mathbf{P} = \operatorname{diag}(1, 1, -1, 1)$.
  4. Transmitted radiation splits between the reciprocal diffuse profile $E_{s'}(\mu_o) / \pi$ (weight $1 - \eta_t$) and a normalized Henyey–Greenstein-like forward-peaked mesophyll lobe (weight $\eta_t \in [0, 1)$, sharpness $\gamma_t > 0$) symmetric in $\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{in}} \cdot \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{out}}$, preserving exact cross-face BTDF reciprocity $f_{t,\mathrm{ad}\to\mathrm{ab}}(\Omega_1 \to \Omega_2) = f_{t,\mathrm{ab}\to\mathrm{ad}}(-\Omega_2 \to -\Omega_1)$ and exact hemispherical integration (leaf_bsdf_spectrum).

Canopy scattering integration

Canopy scattering kernels consume these directional and dorsiventral leaf models at two levels of fidelity:

  • SpecularCanopyScattering and compute_reflection_mueller evaluate leaf-angle-averaged microfacet reflection in the right-handed canopy meridian-plane Stokes basis $(\hat{\mathbf{e}}_s, \hat{\mathbf{e}}_p, \mathbf{k})$ (with $+Q$ along $\hat{\mathbf{e}}_s$ perpendicular to the meridian plane) using singularity-free Cartesian specular bisectors (getSpecularΩ), preserving Hovenier 4×4 reciprocity and $m \ge 1$ $Q \leftrightarrow U$ Fourier couplings.
  • DorsiventralBiLambertianCanopyScattering and compute_Z_matrices_directional provide closed-form Fourier moments for dorsiventral bi-Lambertian leaves ($R_{\mathrm{ad}} \neq R_{\mathrm{ab}}$), returning all four directional blocks $(Z^{++}, Z^{-+}, Z^{+-}, Z^{--})$ and separate downward/upward stream single-scattering albedos $(\omega_{\mathrm{down}}, \omega_{\mathrm{up}})$.
  • compute_Z_matrices_coupled_bsdf projects a prepared PreparedLeafBSDFSpectrum over leaf inclination and azimuth onto the four canopy Fourier phase-matrix blocks while preserving exact passive stream albedo bounds $0 \le \omega_{\mathrm{down}}, \omega_{\mathrm{up}} \le 1$ and 4-block canopy Helmholtz reciprocity.
