Leaf Optics, Surfaces & Coupled BSDF

Leaf-level elastic radiative transfer in CanopyOptics separates tabulated spectral optical constants from leaf structural/biochemical traits, dielectric surface boundaries, and directional BSDF assembly:

  1. Optical tables (LeafOpticalProperties, MultiPigmentOpticalProperties, load_wax_refractive_index) resample refractive indices and specific absorption spectra onto a caller-chosen wavelength grid.
  2. Hemispherical mesophyll & dorsiventral budgets (prospect_budget, leaf_sip_budget, dorsiventral_budget, single_slab_dorsiventral_budget) compute directional-hemispherical or bi-hemispherical reflectance $R$, transmittance $T$, and directly integrated nonnegative material absorptance $A$ ($R + T + A = 1$).
  3. Dielectric boundary models (ConeSurface, RoughDielectricSurface) evaluate smooth Stern–Allen cone transmittance or Beckmann microfacet reflection/transmission with energy-conserving multiple-scattering compensation.
  4. Coupled $4\times 4$ polarized leaf BSDF (CoupledLeafBSDF, prepare_leaf_bsdf, leaf_bsdf_spectrum) couples specular surface reflection ($4\times 4$ Fresnel Mueller matrix) with interface-transmitted diffuse interior transport while preserving Helmholtz reciprocity and directional energy conservation $R_{\mathrm{dir}}(\mu_{\mathrm{in}}) + T_{\mathrm{dir}}(\mu_{\mathrm{in}}) + A_{\mathrm{dir}}(\mu_{\mathrm{in}}) = 1$.

Spectral Optical Tables & Wax Dispersion

CanopyOptics.LeafOpticalProperties — Type
LeafOpticalProperties(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm"; FT = ...)
LeafOpticalProperties{FT}(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm")
LeafOpticalProperties{FT,FT2}(λ, nᵣ, Kcab, Kcar, Kant, Kb, Kw, Km, Kp, Kcbc)

Store spectral grid λ, aqueous mesophyll refractive index nᵣ, and PROSPECT-PRO specific absorption coefficient vectors (Féret et al., 2021).

Calling LeafOpticalProperties(λ_bnds) (or LeafOpticalProperties() for the native 1 nm grid on 400–2500 nm) bin-averages the bundled PROSPECT-PRO optical database over bin edges λ_bnds, accepting any Unitful spectral unit (u"nm", u"μm", u"cm^-1") or unitless values in nm.

Fields

  • λ::FT2: spectral bin centers (with or without Unitful units).
  • nᵣ::Vector{FT}: refractive index of aqueous leaf mesophyll.
  • Kcab::Vector{FT}: pooled chlorophyll a+b specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kcar::Vector{FT}: carotenoid specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kant::Vector{FT}: anthocyanin specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kb::Vector{FT}: senescent brown pigment specific absorption (arbitrary units).
  • Kw::Vector{FT}: liquid water specific absorption (cm⁻¹).
  • Km::Vector{FT}: bulk dry matter specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).
  • Kp::Vector{FT}: protein specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).
  • Kcbc::Vector{FT}: carbon-based constituent specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).

Examples

julia> using Unitful

julia> opti = LeafOpticalProperties((400.0:100.0:2400.0)u"nm");

julia> length(opti.nᵣ)
20

CanopyOptics.load_wax_refractive_index — Function
load_wax_refractive_index(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm"; FT = ...)

Load the Stuckens et al. (2009, Remote Sens. Environ. 113(12), 2560–2573; DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2009.07.014) epicuticular leaf wax refractive-index dispersion spectrum $n_{\mathrm{wax}}(\lambda)$ (data/epicuticular_wax_stuckens2009.csv) averaged onto λ_bnds.

Unlike aqueous internal leaf refractive index $n_r(\lambda)$ (which drops from $\sim 1.42$ in the VNIR to $\sim 1.27$ near $2450\;\mathrm{nm}$ due to liquid-water dispersion), epicuticular wax exhibits weak hydrocarbon dispersion ($n_{\mathrm{wax}} \approx 1.48$ at $400\;\mathrm{nm}$ to $1.40$ at $2500\;\mathrm{nm}$), sustaining cuticular surface specular reflectance in the SWIR when passed via n_surface to prospect_budget, dorsiventral_budget, or prepare_leaf_bsdf.

Mesophyll Plate & Leaf-SIP Budgets

CanopyOptics.LeafProspectProProperties — Type
LeafProspectProProperties{FT}(; N=1.4, Ccab=40.0, Ccar=10.0, Canth=0.5,
                              Cbrown=0.0, Cw=0.012, Cm=..., Cprot=..., Ccbc=...)

Store leaf biochemical and structural state for PROSPECT-D (bulk dry matter Cm mode) and PROSPECT-PRO (Cprot + Ccbc dry-matter partition mode).

When constructed with keywords:

  • Omitting Cm selects PROSPECT-PRO mode (Cm = 0, defaulting Cprot = 0.001 and Ccbc = 0.009).
  • Supplying Cm explicitly (including Cm = 0 or a ForwardDiff.Dual) selects PROSPECT-D mode (Cprot = 0, Ccbc = 0) so dry matter is not double-counted.
  • Setting both Cm > 0 and Cprot > 0 (or Ccbc > 0) throws an ArgumentError.

Fields

  • N::FT: leaf mesophyll structure parameter (N >= 1).
  • Ccab::FT: chlorophyll a+b content in µg cm⁻².
  • Ccar::FT: carotenoid content in µg cm⁻².
  • Canth::FT: anthocyanin content in µg cm⁻² (Féret et al., 2017/2021).
  • Cbrown::FT: senescent brown pigment content (arbitrary units).
  • Cw::FT: equivalent water thickness in cm.
  • Cm::FT: bulk dry matter content (LMA) in g cm⁻² (0 in Cprot + Ccbc mode).
  • Cprot::FT: protein content in g cm⁻².
  • Ccbc::FT: carbon-based constituents content in g cm⁻².

Examples

julia> leaf = LeafProspectProProperties(N = 1.6, Ccab = 45.0, Cm = 0.008);

julia> (leaf.Cm, leaf.Cprot, leaf.Ccbc)
(0.008, 0.0, 0.0)

CanopyOptics.prospect_budget — Function
prospect_budget(leaf, optis)

Compute PROSPECT-PRO transmission, reflection, and directly integrated material absorption, returning (; transmission, reflectance, absorptance). Unlike $1 - R - T$, the local absorptance remains accurate in the nonabsorbing and weak-absorption limits without clipping or rescaling. prospect retains its (T, R) return interface. Absorptance is the monochromatic fraction of incident energy (equivalently photon count) absorbed by all material, not chlorophyll-only absorption or energy retained after fluorescence emission.

Requires finite $N \ge 1$, finite nonnegative constituent contents and absorption coefficients, and refractive indices $n_r > 1$. Uses the PROSPECT first-surface $40^\circ$ and internal diffuse $90^\circ$ Stern–Allen Fresnel conventions; the nearly index-matched limit ($n_r \to 1^+$) is rejected if computed surface transmissions leave $(0, 1]$. Supports CPU scalars/arrays and first-order ForwardDiff.Dual inputs (the absorption Hessian is singular at zero optical depth).

source
prospect_budget(leaf, optics, surface::AbstractLeafSurface;
                μ_in = one(FT), n_surface = nothing)

Compute a generalized PROSPECT plate budget accepting any AbstractLeafSurface (ConeSurface or RoughDielectricSurface), optional directional incidence cosine $\mu_{\mathrm{in}} \in (0, 1]$, and optional outer cuticular refractive-index spectrum n_surface (e.g., load_wax_refractive_index), for either LeafProspectProProperties or LeafMultiPigmentProperties.

First-surface entry uses surface_interface_optics(surface, μ_in, nr; n_surface) and symmetric diffuse plate interfaces use $t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}} / n_r^2$ across all $N \ge 1$ plates (smoothly supporting $1 \le N < 2$), reducing identically to prospect_budget(leaf, optics) when surface == ConeSurface(40, 1) and n_surface === nothing. For distinct outer-epidermis vs internal aqueous cell-wall interfaces, see DorsiventralLeafProperties. Returns (; transmission, reflectance, absorptance, reflectance_volume).

prospect_constituent_coefficients(leaf, optics)

Host-side PROSPECT-PRO composition products for each spectral bin, named by physical constituent. These are the exact concentration × specific-absorption products used by prospect_budget, before dividing their sum by the common plate count N. Dorsiventral & Asymmetric-Surface Leaf Stacks

CanopyOptics.DorsiventralLeafProperties — Type
DorsiventralLeafProperties{LP<:AbstractLeafProperties, LS<:AbstractLeafProperties, SAd<:AbstractLeafSurface, SAb<:AbstractLeafSurface} <: AbstractLeafProperties

Two-zone dorsiventral leaf representation separating palisade (adaxial) and spongy (abaxial) mesophyll layers (palisade, spongy, each a LeafProspectProProperties or LeafMultiPigmentProperties) bounded by distinct adaxial (surface_adaxial) and abaxial (surface_abaxial) dielectric surfaces.

Supported structural domains ($N_{\mathrm{tot}} = N_{\mathrm{pal}} + N_{\mathrm{spo}}$):

  • $N_{\mathrm{pal}} \ge 1$ and $N_{\mathrm{spo}} \ge 1$ ($N_{\mathrm{tot}} \ge 2$): exact two-zone Redheffer plate stack.
  • $0 < N_{\mathrm{pal}} \le 1$, $0 < N_{\mathrm{spo}} \le 1$, and $1 \le N_{\mathrm{tot}} < 2$: continuous convex bridge

    \[\mathcal{B}(N_{\mathrm{tot}}) = (2 - N_{\mathrm{tot}})\,\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{slab}} + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 1)\,\mathcal{B}_{1,1}\]

    between the unbroken $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 1$ two-sublayer slab and the $N_{\mathrm{pal}} = N_{\mathrm{spo}} = 1$ ($N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$) stack, preserving strict energy conservation, nonnegative zone absorptances, and bi-hemispherical reciprocity $T_{\mathrm{ad}} = T_{\mathrm{ab}}$ across thin/compact leaves ($C^0$ continuous at $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$).
  • When single_slab == true, evaluates the unbroken $N = 1$ two-sublayer operator single_slab_dorsiventral_budget directly for any positive $(N_{\mathrm{pal}}, N_{\mathrm{spo}})$.

See dorsiventral_from_traits to construct a dorsiventral leaf smoothly from whole-leaf traits across all $N_{\mathrm{tot}} \ge 1$.

  • Water and dry matter (Cw, Cm, Cprot, Ccbc) are allocated by $f_m \in (0, 1)$ (mesophyll_fraction_palisade).
  • Zone plate counts $(N_{\mathrm{pal}}, N_{\mathrm{spo}})$ satisfy $N_{\mathrm{pal}} + N_{\mathrm{spo}} = N_{\mathrm{tot}}$ and meet continuously at $(1, 1)$ across $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$ ($C^0$ continuous at $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$, $C^\infty$ on $(1, 2)$ and $(2, \infty)$):

    \[(N_{\mathrm{pal}},\, N_{\mathrm{spo}}) = \begin{cases} \bigl(f_m + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 1)(1 - f_m),\; (1 - f_m) + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 1) f_m\bigr), & 1 \le N_{\mathrm{tot}} < 2, \\[4pt] \bigl(1 + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 2) f_m,\; 1 + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 2)(1 - f_m)\bigr), & N_{\mathrm{tot}} \ge 2. \end{cases}\]

CanopyOptics.surface_interface_optics — Function
surface_interface_optics(surface::AbstractLeafSurface, μ_in, nr;
                         hemispherical = false, n_surface = nr)

Evaluate external entry and internal diffuse exit probabilities across a leaf's external dielectric boundary:

  • r_ext, t_ext ($= 1 - r_{\mathrm{ext}}$): external reflectance and entry transmittance (directional at $\mu_{\mathrm{in}}$ when hemispherical == false, or bi-hemispherical $2\pi \to 2\pi$ when hemispherical == true), evaluated at outer cuticular refractive index $n_{\mathrm{eff}} = n_{\mathrm{scale}} \, n_{\mathrm{surface}}$.
  • t_hem_ext: bi-hemispherical ($2\pi \to 2\pi$) external transmittance across surface.
  • t_int_diffuse, r_int_diffuse: diffuse internal-to-air exit transmittance $t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}} / n_r^2$ and internal reflectance $1 - t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}}$ from the aqueous mesophyll ($n_r > 1$) into air ($n = 1$).

Physical thin-cuticle approximation when n_surface != nr (e.g., Stuckens et al. 2009 epicuticular wax $n_{\mathrm{wax}}(\lambda)$ via load_wax_refractive_index): across air ($n = 1$) — lossless thin wax cuticle ($n_{\mathrm{eff}}$) — aqueous mesophyll ($n_r$), smooth internal wax–mesophyll Fresnel reflection is $(n_{\mathrm{eff}} - n_r)^2 / (n_{\mathrm{eff}} + n_r)^2$ ($\approx 0.03$–$0.05\%$ in the VNIR and $\le 0.5\%$ at the $2450\text{ nm}$ SWIR $n_r \approx 1.27$ minimum), with zero wax-to-mesophyll total internal reflection for externally entering rays since $\sin\theta_{\mathrm{wax}} \le 1 / n_{\mathrm{eff}} < n_r / n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ for all $n_r > 1$. Neglecting that $< 0.5\%$ secondary internal bounce, external entry is governed by the air–wax interface ($n_{\mathrm{eff}}$), while diffuse internal exit from the aqueous mesophyll ($n_r$) into air ($n = 1$) obeys exact isothermal detailed-balance étendue invariance $t_{\mathrm{int,diffuse}} = t_{\mathrm{hem,ext}}(n_{\mathrm{eff}}) / n_r^2 \in (0, 1)$.

source
CanopyOptics.calctav_smooth — Function
calctav_smooth(α_deg, nr)

Evaluate cancellation-free Stern (1964) / Allen (1973) cone-averaged dielectric surface transmittance for $\alpha_{\mathrm{deg}} \in [0, 90]$ and $n_r > 1$, supporting Float32, Float64, and ForwardDiff.Dual without precision promotion or $0/0$ singularities at $\alpha_{\mathrm{deg}} = 0^\circ$ or $90^\circ$:

  • For $\sin^2\alpha \le 0.04$ ($\alpha_{\mathrm{deg}} \le 11.54^\circ$, including $0^\circ$ and $8^\circ$), evaluates the strictly positive Fresnel cone integral $\int_0^1 T_F(\xi\sin^2\alpha, n_r)\,\mathrm{d}\xi$ by 5-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature ($< 2\text{ ulp}$ in Float32/Float64, $C^\infty$ at $0^\circ$).
  • Above $0.04$ (including $40^\circ$ and $90^\circ$), evaluates the exact Stern–Allen closed form using $b_1 = \cos\alpha\sqrt{n_r^2 - \sin^2\alpha}$, avoiding $\sqrt{0}$ derivative singularities at $\alpha_{\mathrm{deg}} = 90^\circ$.

CanopyOptics.fresnel_components — Function
fresnel_components(n, θᵢ)

Compute real-dielectric Fresnel amplitude reflectances (r_s, r_p) for an air-to-medium interface (n_air = 1, medium refractive index n >= 1) at incidence angle θᵢ in radians (Born & Wolf, Principles of Optics, Eqs. 1.5.45–1.5.46):

\[r_s = \frac{\cos\theta_i - n\cos\theta_t}{\cos\theta_i + n\cos\theta_t}, \qquad r_p = \frac{n\cos\theta_i - \cos\theta_t}{n\cos\theta_i + \cos\theta_t},\]

where $\sin\theta_t = \sin\theta_i / n$.

julia> (round(rs; digits = 4), round(rp; digits = 4))
(-0.2, 0.2)

CanopyOptics.K — Function
K(κ, α)

Compute the Nilson–Kuusk leaf-surface roughness reduction factor:

\[K(\kappa, \alpha) = e^{-\kappa \tan |\alpha|} .\]

  • $\kappa \approx 0.1\text{–}0.3$ controls surface roughness (κ = 0 gives a smooth Fresnel surface).
  • α is the incidence half-angle in radians, with $|\alpha| \le \pi/2$.
  • At grazing incidence ($|\alpha| = \pi/2$), returns 0 for κ > 0 and 1 for κ = 0 without evaluating tan(Float32(π/2)).

CanopyOptics.CoupledLeafBSDF — Type
CoupledLeafBSDF(leaf, surface; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)
CoupledLeafBSDF(dorsiventral_leaf; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)
CoupledLeafBSDF(asymmetric_surface_leaf; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)

Energy-conserving, Helmholtz-reciprocal, Stokes-polarized leaf bidirectional scattering distribution function (BRDF + BTDF + Mueller matrix) coupling rough dielectric microfacet surface optics consistently with internal plate transport:

  1. First-surface reflection ($\mathbf{M}_{r,\mathrm{surf}}$): Beckmann normal distribution ($\tan^2\theta_h = (H_x^2 + H_y^2) / H_z^2$), erfcx-based Smith shadowing–masking $G_2 = G_1(\mu_i)\,G_1(\mu_o)$, trigonometric-free Fresnel amplitudes, and algebraic meridian-plane Stokes rotations (npol in $\{1, 3, 4\}$), obeying exact Hovenier reciprocity $\mathbf{M}(\boldsymbol{\omega}_o \to \boldsymbol{\omega}_i) = \mathbf{P}\,\mathbf{M}(\boldsymbol{\omega}_i \to \boldsymbol{\omega}_o)^\top \mathbf{P}$ with $\mathbf{P} = \operatorname{diag}(1, 1, -1, 1)$.
  2. Internal volume BRDF ($f_{r,\mathrm{vol}}$): Coupled through external entry $T_{\mathrm{ext},s}(\mu_i) = 1 - R_{\mathrm{surf},s}(\mu_i)$ and directional escape $E_s(\mu_o) = T_{\mathrm{ext},s}(\mu_o) / T_{\mathrm{hem,ext},s}$ using the same RoughDielectricSurface (never a mismatched ConeSurface).
  3. Directional transmission ($f_t$): Symmetric Kelemen–Szirmay-Kalos / Kulla–Conty closure combining diffuse escape ($1 - \eta_t$) with a normalized forward lobe ($\eta_t \in [0, 1)$, concentration $\gamma_t \ge 0$), guaranteeing strict nonnegativity, exact hemispherical conservation $R(\mu_i) + T(\mu_i) + A(\mu_i) = 1$, and exact directional Helmholtz reciprocity $f_t(\boldsymbol{\Omega}_{\mathrm{in}} \to \boldsymbol{\Omega}_{\mathrm{out}}) = f_t(-\boldsymbol{\Omega}_{\mathrm{out}} \to -\boldsymbol{\Omega}_{\mathrm{in}})$.
