Leaf Optics, Surfaces & Coupled BSDF

Leaf-level elastic radiative transfer in CanopyOptics separates tabulated spectral optical constants from leaf structural/biochemical traits, dielectric surface boundaries, and directional BSDF assembly:

  1. Optical tables (LeafOpticalProperties, MultiPigmentOpticalProperties, load_wax_refractive_index) resample refractive indices and specific absorption spectra onto a caller-chosen wavelength grid.
  2. Hemispherical mesophyll & dorsiventral budgets (prospect_budget, leaf_sip_budget, dorsiventral_budget, single_slab_dorsiventral_budget) compute directional-hemispherical or bi-hemispherical reflectance R, transmittance T, and directly integrated nonnegative material absorptance A (R + T + A = 1).
  3. Dielectric boundary models (ConeSurface, RoughDielectricSurface) evaluate smooth Stern–Allen cone transmittance or Beckmann microfacet reflection/transmission with energy-conserving multiple-scattering compensation.
  4. Coupled 4×4 polarized leaf BSDF (CoupledLeafBSDF, prepare_leaf_bsdf, leaf_bsdf_spectrum) couples specular surface reflection (4×4 Fresnel Mueller matrix) with interface-transmitted diffuse interior transport while preserving Helmholtz reciprocity and directional energy conservation R_dir(μ_in) + T_dir(μ_in) + A_dir(μ_in) = 1.

Spectral Optical Tables & Wax Dispersion

CanopyOptics.LeafOpticalProperties — Type
LeafOpticalProperties(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm"; FT = ...)
LeafOpticalProperties{FT}(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm")
LeafOpticalProperties{FT,FT2}(λ, nᵣ, Kcab, Kcar, Kant, Kb, Kw, Km, Kp, Kcbc)

Store spectral grid λ, aqueous mesophyll refractive index nᵣ, and PROSPECT-PRO specific absorption coefficient vectors (Féret et al., 2021).

Calling LeafOpticalProperties(λ_bnds) (or LeafOpticalProperties() for the native 1 nm grid on 400–2500 nm) bin-averages the bundled PROSPECT-PRO optical database over bin edges λ_bnds, accepting any Unitful spectral unit (u"nm", u"μm", u"cm^-1") or unitless values in nm.

Fields

  • λ::FT2: spectral bin centers (with or without Unitful units).
  • nᵣ::Vector{FT}: refractive index of aqueous leaf mesophyll.
  • Kcab::Vector{FT}: pooled chlorophyll a+b specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kcar::Vector{FT}: carotenoid specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kant::Vector{FT}: anthocyanin specific absorption (cm² µg⁻¹).
  • Kb::Vector{FT}: senescent brown pigment specific absorption (arbitrary units).
  • Kw::Vector{FT}: liquid water specific absorption (cm⁻¹).
  • Km::Vector{FT}: bulk dry matter specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).
  • Kp::Vector{FT}: protein specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).
  • Kcbc::Vector{FT}: carbon-based constituent specific absorption (cm² g⁻¹).

Examples

julia> using Unitful

julia> opti = LeafOpticalProperties((400.0:100.0:2400.0)u"nm");

julia> length(opti.nᵣ)
20

CanopyOptics.load_wax_refractive_index — Function
load_wax_refractive_index(λ_bnds = (399.5:2500.5)u"nm"; FT = ...)

Load the Stuckens et al. (2009, Remote Sens. Environ. 113(12), 2560–2573; DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2009.07.014) epicuticular leaf wax refractive-index dispersion spectrum n_wax(λ) (data/epicuticular_wax_stuckens2009.csv) averaged onto λ_bnds.

Unlike aqueous internal leaf refractive index nᵣ(λ) (which drops from ~1.42 in VNIR to ~1.27 near 2450 nm due to liquid-water dispersion), epicuticular wax exhibits weak hydrocarbon dispersion (n_wax ≈ 1.48 at 400 nm to 1.40 at 2500 nm), sustaining cuticular surface specular reflectance in the SWIR when passed via n_surface to prospect_budget, dorsiventral_budget, or prepare_leaf_bsdf.

Mesophyll Plate & Leaf-SIP Budgets

CanopyOptics.LeafProspectProProperties — Type
LeafProspectProProperties{FT}(; N=1.4, Ccab=40.0, Ccar=10.0, Canth=0.5,
                              Cbrown=0.0, Cw=0.012, Cm=..., Cprot=..., Ccbc=...)

Store leaf biochemical and structural state for PROSPECT-D (bulk dry matter Cm mode) and PROSPECT-PRO (Cprot + Ccbc dry-matter partition mode).

When constructed with keywords:

  • Omitting Cm selects PROSPECT-PRO mode (Cm = 0, defaulting Cprot = 0.001 and Ccbc = 0.009).
  • Supplying Cm explicitly (including Cm = 0 or a ForwardDiff.Dual) selects PROSPECT-D mode (Cprot = 0, Ccbc = 0) so dry matter is not double-counted.
  • Setting both Cm > 0 and Cprot > 0 (or Ccbc > 0) throws an ArgumentError.

Fields

  • N::FT: leaf mesophyll structure parameter (N >= 1).
  • Ccab::FT: chlorophyll a+b content in µg cm⁻².
  • Ccar::FT: carotenoid content in µg cm⁻².
  • Canth::FT: anthocyanin content in µg cm⁻² (Féret et al., 2017/2021).
  • Cbrown::FT: senescent brown pigment content (arbitrary units).
  • Cw::FT: equivalent water thickness in cm.
  • Cm::FT: bulk dry matter content (LMA) in g cm⁻² (0 in Cprot + Ccbc mode).
  • Cprot::FT: protein content in g cm⁻².
  • Ccbc::FT: carbon-based constituents content in g cm⁻².

Examples

julia> leaf = LeafProspectProProperties(N = 1.6, Ccab = 45.0, Cm = 0.008);

julia> (leaf.Cm, leaf.Cprot, leaf.Ccbc)
(0.008, 0.0, 0.0)

CanopyOptics.prospect_budget — Function
prospect_budget(leaf, optis)

PROSPECT-PRO transmission, reflection and directly integrated material absorption. Returns a named tuple of spectral arrays. Unlike 1-R-T, the local absorptance remains meaningful in the nonabsorbing/weak-absorption limit. No flux is clipped or rescaled. prospect retains its (T, R) return interface. Absorptance is the monochromatic fraction of incident energy (equivalently photon count) absorbed by all material, not chlorophyll-only absorption or energy retained after fluorescence emission.

Requires finite N >= 1, finite nonnegative constituent contents/absorption coefficients and refractive indices > 1. Both prospect and prospect_budget enforce N >= 1. The PROSPECT first-surface 40-degree and internal diffuse 90-degree conventions and Fresnel routine are used. Its accuracy is tested across the optical database's index range; the nearly index-matched limit is ill-conditioned and is not covered by that accuracy claim. Invalid computed surface transmissions are rejected. CPU and first-order ForwardDiff paths are supported; this function does not claim CUDA execution. The physical absorption Hessian is singular at exactly zero material optical depth.

source
prospect_budget(leaf, optics, surface::AbstractLeafSurface;
                μ_in = one(FT), n_surface = nothing)

Generalized PROSPECT plate budget accepting any AbstractLeafSurface (ConeSurface or RoughDielectricSurface), optional directional incidence cosine μ_in ∈ (0, 1], and optional outer cuticular refractive-index spectrum n_surface (e.g., load_wax_refractive_index), for either LeafProspectProProperties or LeafMultiPigmentProperties.

Note: First-surface entry uses surface_interface_optics(surface, μ_in, nr; n_surface) and symmetric diffuse plate interfaces use t_int_diffuse = t_hem_ext / nr² across all N >= 1 plates (smoothly supporting 1 <= N < 2), reducing identically to legacy prospect_budget when surface == ConeSurface(40, 1) and n_surface === nothing. For distinct outer-epidermis vs internal aqueous cell-wall interfaces, see DorsiventralLeafProperties. Returns (; transmission, reflectance, absorptance, reflectance_volume).

CanopyOptics.prospect_constituent_coefficients — Function
prospect_constituent_coefficients(leaf, optics)

Host-side PROSPECT-PRO composition products for each spectral bin, named by physical constituent. These are the exact concentration × specific-absorption products used by prospect_budget, before dividing their sum by the common plate count N. That common scale cancels in homogeneous constituent attribution.

No spectrum, plant profile or units are selected here. Contents and coefficients must use the matching units documented by LeafProspectProProperties and LeafOpticalProperties. Bulk dry matter overlaps protein/carbon constituents; set Cm=0 when those PRO constituents already describe the same mass. Validation occurs in prospect_budget and the scalar partition_absorption kernel. This array assembly is separate from the CPU/GPU scalar partition/selection rules.

Dorsiventral & Asymmetric-Surface Leaf Stacks

CanopyOptics.DorsiventralLeafProperties — Type
DorsiventralLeafProperties{LP<:AbstractLeafProperties, LS<:AbstractLeafProperties, SAd<:AbstractLeafSurface, SAb<:AbstractLeafSurface} <: AbstractLeafProperties

Two-zone dorsiventral leaf representation separating palisade (adaxial) and spongy (abaxial) mesophyll layers (palisade, spongy, each a LeafProspectProProperties or LeafMultiPigmentProperties) bounded by distinct adaxial (surface_adaxial) and abaxial (surface_abaxial) dielectric surfaces.

Supported structural domains (N_tot = palisade.N + spongy.N):

  • N_pal >= 1 and N_spo >= 1 (N_tot >= 2): exact two-zone Redheffer stack.
  • 0 < N_pal <= 1, 0 < N_spo <= 1, and 1 <= N_tot < 2: continuous convex bridge (2 - N_tot) * B_slab + (N_tot - 1) * B_two_plate between the unbroken N_tot = 1 two-sublayer slab and the N_pal = N_spo = 1 (N_tot = 2) stack, preserving strict energy conservation, zone absorptances, and T_ad == T_ab across thin/compact leaves (1 <= N_tot < 2, C^0 continuous at N_tot = 2).
  • When single_slab == true, evaluates the unbroken N = 1 two-sublayer operator single_slab_dorsiventral_budget directly for any positive (N_pal, N_spo).

See dorsiventral_from_traits to construct a dorsiventral leaf smoothly from whole-leaf traits across all N_tot >= 1.

CanopyOptics.dorsiventral_from_traits — Function
dorsiventral_from_traits(whole_leaf; N_tot = whole_leaf.N,

                         surface_adaxial = ConeSurface(40),

                         single_slab = false)

Partition a whole-leaf LeafProspectProProperties or LeafMultiPigmentProperties into a DorsiventralLeafProperties over all continuous total plate counts N_tot >= 1:

  • Photosynthetic and accessory pigments (Ccab or Cchla, Cchlb, Ccar, Canth, Cbrown) are allocated by fc = pigment_fraction_palisade ∈ [0, 1] (palisade = fc * C, spongy = (1 - fc) * C).
  • Water and dry matter (Cw, Cm, Cprot, Ccbc) are allocated by fm = mesophyll_fraction_palisade ∈ (0, 1).
  • Zone plate counts (N_pal, N_spo) satisfy N_pal + N_spo == N_tot and meet continuously at (1, 1) across N_tot = 2 (C^0 continuous at N_tot = 2, C^∞ on (1, 2) and (2, ∞)):
    • For 1 <= N_tot < 2: N_pal = fm + (N_tot - 1)*(1 - fm), N_spo = (1 - fm) + (N_tot - 1)*fm.
    • For N_tot >= 2: N_pal = 1 + (N_tot - 2)*fm, N_spo = 1 + (N_tot - 2)*(1 - fm).

CanopyOptics.ConeSurface — Type
ConeSurface(α_deg = 40, n_scale = 1)

Isotropic incidence-cone dielectric boundary generalizing PROSPECT's Stern–Allen surface (α_deg = 40 reproduces legacy PROSPECT; α_deg = 0 models collimated nadir integrating-sphere illumination; α_deg = 8 models 8°-port integrating spheres). n_scale scales the aqueous refractive index nr at the external epicuticular film (n_eff = n_scale * nr), while internal diffuse exit into the common nr mesophyll obeys exact 1 / nr² étendue invariance.

CanopyOptics.surface_interface_optics — Function
surface_interface_optics(surface::AbstractLeafSurface, μ_in, nr;
                         hemispherical = false, n_surface = nr)

Evaluate external entry and internal diffuse exit probabilities across a leaf's external dielectric boundary:

  • r_ext, t_ext = 1 - r_ext: external reflectance and entry transmittance (directional at μ_in when hemispherical == false, or hemispherical 2π -> 2π when hemispherical == true), evaluated at outer cuticular refractive index n_eff = surface.n_scale * n_surface.
  • t_hem_ext: hemispherical (2π -> 2π) external transmittance across surface.
  • t_int_diffuse = t_hem_ext / nr², r_int_diffuse = 1 - t_int_diffuse: diffuse internal-to-air exit transmittance and internal reflectance from the aqueous mesophyll (nr > 1) into air (n = 1).

Physical thin-cuticle approximation when n_surface != nr (e.g., Stuckens et al. 2009 epicuticular wax n_wax(λ) via load_wax_refractive_index): across air (n = 1) — lossless thin wax cuticle (n_eff) — aqueous mesophyll (nr), smooth internal wax–mesophyll Fresnel reflection is (n_eff - nr)² / (n_eff + nr)² (≈ 0.03–0.05% in VNIR and ≤ 0.5% at the 2450 nm SWIR nr ≈ 1.27 minimum), with zero wax→mesophyll total internal reflection for externally entering rays since sin θ_wax ≤ 1 / n_eff < nr / n_eff for all nr > 1. Neglecting that < 0.5% secondary internal wax–mesophyll bounce, external entry is governed by the high-contrast air–wax interface (n_eff), while diffuse internal exit from the aqueous mesophyll (nr) into air (n = 1) obeys exact isothermal detailed-balance étendue invariance t_int_diffuse = t_hem_ext(n_eff) / nr² ∈ (0, 1) strictly.

CanopyOptics.calctav_smooth — Function
calctav_smooth(α_deg, nr)

Cancellation-free Stern (1964) / Allen (1973) cone-averaged dielectric surface transmittance for α_deg ∈ [0, 90] and nr > 1, supporting Float32, Float64, and ForwardDiff.Dual without precision promotion or 0/0 singularities at α_deg = 0 or α_deg = 90:

  • For sind(α_deg)^2 <= 0.04 (α_deg <= 11.54°, including 0° and 8°), evaluates the defining strictly-positive Fresnel integral ∫_0^1 T_F(sa2*ξ, nr) dξ by 5-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature (< 1-2 ulp in Float32/Float64, C^∞ at 0°).
  • Above 0.04 (including 40° and 90°), evaluates the exact Stern–Allen closed form with _b1 = cosd(α_deg) * sqrt(nr^2 - sa2), avoiding sqrt(0) derivative blowup at α_deg = 90°.

CanopyOptics.fresnel_components — Function
fresnel_components(n, θᵢ)

Compute real-dielectric Fresnel amplitude reflectances (r_s, r_p) for an air-to-medium interface (n_air = 1, medium refractive index n >= 1) at incidence angle θᵢ in radians (Born & Wolf, Principles of Optics, Eqs. 1.5.45–1.5.46):

\[r_s = \frac{\cos\theta_i - n\cos\theta_t}{\cos\theta_i + n\cos\theta_t}, \qquad r_p = \frac{n\cos\theta_i - \cos\theta_t}{n\cos\theta_i + \cos\theta_t},\]

where sin θ_t = sin θ_i / n.

CanopyOptics.K — Function
K(κ, α)

Compute the Nilson–Kuusk leaf-surface roughness reduction factor:

$K(κ, α) = e^{-κ \tan |α|}$

  • κ ≈ 0.1–0.3 controls surface roughness (κ = 0 gives a smooth Fresnel surface).
  • α is the incidence half-angle in radians, with abs(α) ≤ π/2.
  • At grazing incidence (abs(α) = π/2), returns 0 for κ > 0 and 1 for κ = 0 without evaluating tan(Float32(π/2)).

Used in compute_reflection to attenuate specular reflectance for rough leaves.

CanopyOptics.CoupledLeafBSDF — Type
CoupledLeafBSDF(leaf, surface; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)
CoupledLeafBSDF(dorsiventral_leaf; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)
CoupledLeafBSDF(asymmetric_surface_leaf; η_t = 0, γ_t = 4)

Energy-conserving, Helmholtz-reciprocal, Stokes-polarized leaf bidirectional scattering distribution function (BRDF + BTDF + Mueller matrix) coupling rough dielectric microfacet surface optics consistently with internal plate transport:

  1. First-surface reflection (M_{r,surf}): Beckmann normal distribution (tan² θ_h = (H_x² + H_y²) / H_z²), erfcx-based Smith G₂ = G₁(μ_i) G₁(μ_o), trigonometric-free Fresnel amplitudes, and algebraic meridian-plane Stokes rotations (npol ∈ (1, 3, 4)), obeying exact Hovenier reciprocity M(ω_o -> ω_i) = P M(ω_i -> ω_o)ᵀ P (P = diag(1, 1, -1, 1)).
  2. Internal volume BRDF (f_{r,vol}): Coupled through T_{ext,s}(μ_i) = 1 - R_{surf,s}(μ_i) and directional escape E_s(μ_o) = T_{ext,s}(μ_o) / T_{hem,ext,s} using the same RoughDielectricSurface (never a mismatched ConeSurface).
  3. Directional transmission (f_t): Symmetric Kelemen–Szirmay-Kalos / Kulla–Conty closure combining diffuse escape (1 - η_t) with a normalized forward lobe (η_t ∈ [0, 1), concentration γ_t >= 0), guaranteeing strict nonnegativity, exact hemispherical conservation R(μ_i) + T(μ_i) + A(μ_i) == 1, and exact directional Helmholtz reciprocity f_t(Ω_in -> Ω_out) == f_t(-Ω_out -> -Ω_in).

Wavelength-resolved precomputation returned by prepare_leaf_bsdf storing adaxial and abaxial cuticular surface parameters, outer refractive-index spectra (n_surf_ad, n_surf_ab), hemispherical volume/transmission/absorption budgets, 12-node directional escape profiles, and Kelemen–Szirmay-Kalos directional transmission normalization constants. Enables O(1) multi-angle BRDF, BTDF, and Stokes Mueller evaluations via leaf_bsdf_spectrum and leaf_directional_budget.

CanopyOptics.surface_brdf_mueller — Function
surface_brdf_mueller(μ_i, ϕ_i, μ_o, ϕ_o, n_eff, σ, npol = 1)

Single-bounce polarized microfacet BRDF Mueller matrix M_{r,surf} (npol × npol, npol ∈ (1, 3, 4)) in the leaf local frame (ẑ = (0, 0, 1) outward face normal), where (μ_i, ϕ_i) points toward the illumination source (k_in = -ω_i) and (μ_o, ϕ_o) points toward the detector (k_out = +ω_o), with μ_i, μ_o ∈ (0, 1].

In the meridian-plane Stokes convention of CanopyOptics, +Q denotes perpendicular (s / horizontal) linear polarization and -Q denotes parallel (p / in-meridian) linear polarization. All geometry and Stokes rotations are computed algebraically without acos, asin, or atan singularities.

Evaluate the full spectral leaf BSDF at incident source direction (μ_i, ϕ_i) on face ∈ (:adaxial, :abaxial), reflected detector direction (μ_o, ϕ_o) (μ_o > 0 on the illuminated face), and transmitted detector direction (μ_o, ϕ_o) (μ_o > 0 measured from the opposite face normal -ẑ, so straight-through forward continuation of (θ_i, ϕ_i = 0) is (θ_o = θ_i, ϕ_o = π)):

  • brdf: total scalar BRDF [sr⁻¹] (nλ-vector, = brdf_surface + brdf_volume)
  • btdf: total scalar BTDF [sr⁻¹] (nλ-vector)
  • brdf_mueller: (npol, npol, nλ) polarized BRDF Mueller array (I -> I equals brdf; diffuse internal multiple scattering populates (1, 1) unpolarized)
  • dolp: reflected degree of linear polarization hypot(M[2,1], M[3,1]) / M[1,1] when npol >= 3 (for unpolarized incident light), else zeros(FT, nλ).
