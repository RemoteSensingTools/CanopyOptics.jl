Dorsiventral anatomy, multi-pigment bases and two-zone fluorescence

Homogeneous plate models represent a leaf as a single symmetric stack with one pooled chlorophyll $a{+}b$ spectrum and uniform pigment depth distribution. Dicotyledonous leaves violate these assumptions in three distinct ways:

  1. Dorsiventral mesophyll & cuticle asymmetry: Dense columnar palisade parenchyma beneath a smooth, waxy adaxial epidermis concentrates $65$–$85\%$ of leaf chlorophyll, whereas lacunose spongy mesophyll above a rougher stomatal abaxial epidermis increases internal air–cell-wall scattering, making $R_{\mathrm{ab}} > R_{\mathrm{ad}}$ in the visible while preserving $T_{\mathrm{ad}} = T_{\mathrm{ab}}$ under hemispherical illumination.
  2. Distinct chlorophyll $a$ and $b$ absorption bands: Chlorophyll $a$ (Soret near $434\text{ nm}$, $Q_y$ near $668$–$676\text{ nm}$) and chlorophyll $b$ (Soret near $466\text{ nm}$, $Q_y$ near $650\text{ nm}$) differ across blue-green and red-edge wavelengths, affecting both pigment retrievals and photosystem excitation partitioning.
  3. Depth-asymmetric fluorescence reabsorption: Because palisade tissue is optically dense at the $685\text{ nm}$ PSII emission peak and nearly transparent at $740\text{ nm}$, adaxial vs abaxial excitation produces strongly wavelength-dependent front/back escape ratios.

Continuous N_tot >= 1 dorsiventral and asymmetric-surface leaves

DorsiventralLeafProperties couples a palisade sublayer and a spongy sublayer between independent adaxial and abaxial AbstractLeafSurface boundaries via exact Redheffer star-product adding, returning per-face reflectance, transmittance, total absorptance, and separate zone absorptances (absorptance_palisade, absorptance_spongy) through dorsiventral_budget.

Because empirical leaf inversions frequently encounter compact sun leaves or monocots with total effective structure parameter $1 \le N_{\mathrm{tot}} < 2$, dorsiventral_from_traits and DorsiventralLeafProperties bridge smoothly across $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$:

  • For $N_{\mathrm{tot}} \ge 2$: $N_{\mathrm{pal}} = 1 + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 2) f_m$ and $N_{\mathrm{spo}} = 1 + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 2)(1 - f_m)$, evaluated via the two-zone fractional Allen stack.
  • For $1 \le N_{\mathrm{tot}} < 2$: convex interpolation $(2 - N_{\mathrm{tot}}) \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{slab}} + (N_{\mathrm{tot}} - 1) \mathcal{B}_{1,1}$ between the unbroken two-sublayer slab (single_slab_dorsiventral_budget) at $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 1$ and the $(N_{\mathrm{pal}}, N_{\mathrm{spo}}) = (1, 1)$ two-plate stack at $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$.

This bridge is $C^0$ continuous at $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = 2$ ($C^\infty$ on $(1, 2)$ and $(2, \infty)$), strictly passive ($R + T + A = 1$, $A_{\mathrm{pal}} + A_{\mathrm{spo}} = A$), ForwardDiff-compatible, and preserves exact bi-hemispherical reciprocity $T_{\mathrm{ad}} = T_{\mathrm{ab}}$ to machine precision. AsymmetricSurfaceLeafProperties applies the same continuous $N \ge 1$ bridge when only the adaxial and abaxial cuticles differ.

Multi-pigment optical bases and dry-matter conventions

LeafMultiPigmentProperties and MultiPigmentOpticalProperties separate $C_{\mathrm{chla}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{chlb}}$ while retaining carotenoids, anthocyanins, senescent brown pigments, water, and either bulk dry matter (Cm) or PROSPECT-PRO protein + carbon-based constituents (Cprot, Ccbc).

MultiPigmentOpticalProperties(λ_bnds; basis = ...) constructs MultiPigmentOpticalProperties on any wavelength grid with four selectable basis options:

basisPigment spectra (400–800 nm)Units
:calibrated_in_vivoEmpirically separated in-vivo Kchla, Kchlb (ANGERS species-grouped CV) + OPTI_2021 Kcar, Kantcm² µg⁻¹
:prospect_mp_2017Zhang et al. (2017) PROSPECT-MP pseudo-Voigt Kchla, Kchlb, Kcar + OPTI_2021 Kantcm² µg⁻¹
:prospect_mp_plus_2022Zhang et al. (2022) PROSPECT-MP+ pseudo-Voigt Kchla, Kchlb, Kcar, Kantcm² µg⁻¹
:prospect_pro_pooledBaseline Kchla == Kchlb == Kcab from OPTI_2021cm² µg⁻¹

Above $800\text{ nm}$, all four bases splice cleanly onto OPTI_2021 so that unbounded pseudo-Voigt Lorentzian wings cannot leak artificial pigment absorption into the $800$–$2500\text{ nm}$ NIR/SWIR plateau. For excitation partitioning, MultiChlorophyllAntenna weights $C_{\mathrm{chla}}$, $C_{\mathrm{chlb}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{car}}$ separately in photosystem_excitation.

Dry-matter mutual exclusivity and anthocyanin mass units

All three leaf property types (LeafProspectProProperties, LeafMultiPigmentProperties, LeafSIPProperties) enforce two input-consistency safeguards on keyword construction:

  1. No silent Cm + Cprot/Ccbc double-counting: Passing Cm explicitly (PROSPECT-D bulk dry-matter mode, e.g. LeafProspectProProperties(Cm = 0.006)) automatically defaults omitted Cprot and Ccbc to 0 instead of adding the default 0.001 + 0.009 = 0.010 g cm⁻² PROSPECT-PRO dry-matter background on top of Cm. Passing positive Cm together with positive Cprot or Ccbc throws an ArgumentError.
  2. Consistent [µg cm⁻²] anthocyanin units: In OPTI_2021 (dataSpec_PRO.csv, Féret et al., 2017/2021), Kant peaks at $0.0725\text{ cm}^2\,\mu\text{g}^{-1}$ near $535\text{ nm}$; Canth is therefore specified in mass units [µg cm⁻²] across all CanopyOptics leaf models.

Two-zone internal fluorescence escape

DorsiventralPlateEscape and DorsiventralBirthProfile extend leaf_emission_fates from a single homogeneous plate (RegularPlateEscape) to two coupled mesophyll zones (adaxial_depth $b_1$, abaxial_depth $b_2$, outer diffuse exit transmissions $t_1, t_2 \in (0, 1]$, and palisade–spongy internal boundary transmission $t_{\mathrm{int}} \in (0, 1]$).

using CanopyOptics

dpo_exc = dorsiventral_plate_optics(2.4, 0.8, 1.42; internal_air_gap = true)
dpo_685 = dorsiventral_plate_optics(1.8, 0.6, 1.41; internal_air_gap = true)
birth   = DorsiventralBirthProfile(
    0.85, 0.15,
    dpo_exc.adaxial_effective_depth, 1.0, 0.1,
    dpo_exc.abaxial_effective_depth, 1.0, 0.2,
)
fates = leaf_emission_fates(dpo_685.escape, birth)

Local dimensionless depth $\zeta_i \in [0, 1]$ runs anatomically adaxial to abaxial in both zones ($\zeta_1 = 0$ at the adaxial epidermis to $\zeta_1 = 1$ at the internal boundary; $\zeta_2 = 0$ at the internal boundary to $\zeta_2 = 1$ at the abaxial epidermis). Reversing leaf orientation therefore swaps $(w_1, a_1, f_1, g_1) \leftrightarrow (w_2, a_2, g_2, f_2)$ and front $\leftrightarrow$ back while leaving reabsorbed invariant.

When $t_{\mathrm{int}} = 1$ and $t_1 = t_2 = t$, splitting a homogeneous plate $b = \eta b + (1-\eta)b$ at any $\eta \in (0, 1)$ reproduces RegularPlateEscape(b, t) to floating-point roundoff. Setting internal_air_gap = true in dorsiventral_plate_optics uses the multiple-reflection diffuse mesophyll–air–mesophyll transmittance $t_{\mathrm{gap}} = t_{90} / [n_r^2 (2 - t_{90})]$, capturing partial internal back-reflection at the spongy–palisade intercellular air interface. Both DorsiventralPlateEscape and DorsiventralBirthProfile are isbits, allocation-free, subnormal-safe across $(0, 1]^3$, and execute identically on CPU and CUDA (Float32/Float64).
