Pigment absorption and antenna excitation

The optical solver supplies total absorbed radiation. Pigment allocation then answers which material absorbed those photons. Antenna selection answers which absorbed photons transfer excitation into the photosynthetic antenna. Photosystem allocation is a third, separate choice. Keeping these steps separate allows a future leaf optical model or physiological model to replace one component without owning the others.

The core workflow is scalar and applies separately to each wavelength bin:

partition = partition_absorption(HomogeneousAbsorption(), absorbed_photons, coefficients)
antenna = antenna_excitation(selection, partition)
q2 = photosystem_excitation(antenna, psii_fraction)

coefficients is a NamedTuple of concentration × specific-absorption products in a common optical-depth convention. Its values and the photon input use the same floating-point type. The caller supplies the optical model, spectrum, composition, transfer probabilities and photosystem fractions. No species profile, light field or empirical transfer fraction is selected by the core.

Homogeneous material assumption

If every optical path encounters the same constituent proportions, then at one wavelength the fraction of material absorption due to constituent $i$ is

\[Q_i = Q_{\rm abs}\frac{k_i}{\sum_j k_j}.\]

This follows by integrating the species-specific absorption rate $k_i\exp[-(\sum_j k_j)s]$ along any path, then summing paths. It remains valid with multiple reflections when composition is unchanged along those paths. It is not a model of spatially segregated pigments in heterogeneous mesophyll. A different partition rule can implement AbstractAbsorptionPartition when that physics is needed.

HomogeneousAbsorption first scales coefficients by their maximum to avoid summing overflowing products. Normalizing these constituent probabilities is the physical partition definition; it does not alter or normalize total optical absorption. The optical solver must independently close its radiation budget. Do not attribute broadband absorption using a broadband-averaged coefficient ratio: partition the spectral bins before integration.

For PROSPECT-PRO, the host helper prospect_constituent_coefficients(leaf, optics) returns the exact products also used by prospect_budget. It names chlorophyll, carotenoids, anthocyanins, brown material, water, bulk dry matter, protein and carbon constituents. Their common division by plate count cancels in the fractions. Set bulk dry matter Cm=0 when protein/carbon constituents already account for the same mass. For a different optical model, supply a different named tuple directly; no PROSPECT fields or database are required by the scalar partition.

Use prospect_budget(...).absorptance for weak material absorption. The rounded residual 1−R−T cannot retain absorption smaller than the spacing of the separately reported reflection and transmission. See the numerical optical contract.

Antenna and photosystem choices

ChlorophyllOnly() selects chlorophyll absorption. CarotenoidAntenna(eta) selects

\[Q_{\rm ant}=Q_{\rm chl}+\eta_{\rm car}Q_{\rm car},\qquad 0\le\eta_{\rm car}\le1.\]

The carotenoid transfer fraction is a photon-transfer probability. It is not an energy efficiency or the physiological NPQ rate $K_N$. Untransferred carotenoid absorption stays in the material absorption/energy ledger; it is not automatically assigned to a particular heat or quenching pathway. These conventions are consistent with the constituent allocation described by Platespect (2025).

The partition retains unitless constituent fractions as well as photon diagnostics. Built-in selection accumulates the smaller selected or unselected probability and multiplies by total photons, using the complementary probability when appropriate. This equivalent probability identity avoids a one-ulp excess over total photons near complete selection. It neither clips photons nor changes constituent diagnostics. This preserves pigment identity even when constituent photon amounts underflow. At subnormal input magnitudes, individual constituent values may still round to zero; extreme coefficient ratios can also underflow the constituent fractions themselves. No relative-accuracy claim below the selected FT's probability/flux resolution is made.

The separate photosystem allocation computes $Q_2(\lambda)=\alpha_2(\lambda) Q_{\rm ant}(\lambda)$ with an explicit finite fraction in $[0,1]$. The caller then integrates Q2 over a declared spectral domain. A leaf model receiving this already allocated Q2 must not apply another PSII fraction. The same excitation should drive electron transport and the physiological fluorescence source. This optics package introduces no photosynthesis or fluorescence dependency.

Total material photon absorption, antenna excitation, PSII excitation and absorbed energy remain different quantities. Energy requires wavelength-specific photon energy before summation. PAR energy is not the full shortwave energy budget. Use consistent spectral extents, units and reduction conventions for total PAR and Q2. The all-selected $\eta_{\rm car}=\alpha_2=1$ endpoint is tested on both CPU and CUDA, including exact equality of the resulting integrated photon arrays.

Status and extension contracts

StatusMeaning
AbsorptionPartitionValidValid material attribution, including zero illumination of absorbing material
AbsorptionNoMaterialAll coefficient products are zero (or the tuple is empty), and supplied absorption is zero
AbsorptionInvalidInputA photon input or coefficient product is negative or nonfinite
AbsorptionIncompatibleInputNonzero absorption was supplied for material with no absorption coefficients

Invalid results contain NaN numerical payloads. For absent absorption coefficients, constituent fractions are undefined (NaN) although absorbed photons are zero. absorption_valid(result) checks status; the antenna wrapper checks it before calling a selection. Built-in policies return typed NaN for a missing required pigment name, even for an empty partition. ChlorophyllOnly does not require a carotenoid key. photosystem_excitation returns NaN for invalid photon inputs or fractions. Mixed-precision coefficient tuples are not silently converted.

Add a new absorber by adding a name/value to the coefficient NamedTuple. A custom AbstractAntennaSelection implements select_absorbed_photons(selection, partition) and can use that name without changing a central list. It returns a same-FT photon amount, or NaN when required inputs are unavailable. Normally call the public antenna_excitation wrapper, which checks result status and the returned photon bound. Custom selectors remain responsible for the physical meaning and validation of their own parameters.

The implemented kernels are tested in Float32/Float64 on CPU and NVIDIA CUDA, including status propagation, empty/reordered batches and a custom selector. Optical tables and PROSPECT composition are prepared on the host. This does not claim GPU PROSPECT special functions, Metal execution or automatic differentiation of these new partition/selection methods. No canopy grid or persistent individual leaf identity is introduced.
